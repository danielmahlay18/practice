Barcelona's Starting XI

Barcelona coach Hansi Flick has announced which players he'll be using in the 9.30pm CET kick-off at home to Celta this Wednesday, with the team currently six points clear of Real Madrid, who have already played and won their own midweek fixture.

Barcelona are without the injured Ansu Fati, Pedri and Sergi Roberto, while Raphinha is also out with a hamstring injury. Marc Bernal is also unavailable for selection.
